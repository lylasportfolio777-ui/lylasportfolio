# Website Administration Guide: Step-by-Step Instructions

This guide provides exact, click-by-click instructions on how to use your Admin Dashboard to edit every part of your website. 

---

## 🔑 How to Log In
1. Open your web browser and go to: `https://your-website-url.com/login`
2. Enter your Email Address and Password.
3. Click the **Log In** button. You will be taken to your Dashboard.
4. On the left side of your screen, you will see a navigation sidebar with all your website's tabs.

---

## 🏠 How to Edit the Homepage (Hero Section)
*This controls the massive text and background image when people first open your site.*

1. Click on the **Home** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Hero Title**, click inside the text box and type your new main title.
3. Under **Hero Subtitle**, click the text box and type your new subtitle.
4. Under **Hero Main Image**, click the **Change** or **Upload** button inside the dashed box.
5. Select a photo from your computer.
6. Wait a few seconds for the green **Uploaded** checkmark to appear.
7. Click the **Save All Settings** button at the top right of your screen.

---

## ⏳ How to Edit the Preloader (Loading Screen)
*This controls the fast-flashing images and text that appear while the site loads.*

1. Click on the **Preloader** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Preloader Central Text**, click the text box to change the loading text.
3. You will see 6 Image Upload boxes (Preloader Image 1 through 6).
4. To replace an image, click **Change** inside its dashed box and select a file from your computer.
5. To remove an image completely without replacing it, click the red **Delete** button.
6. Once you are finished, click the **Save All Settings** button at the top right.

---

## 📂 How to Add Categories for Your Portfolio
*You must create Categories before you can upload projects.*

1. Click on the **Categories** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Click the text box that says "New Category Name".
3. Type the name of your category (e.g., "Maternity" or "Couples").
4. Click the **Add Category** button.
5. The category will instantly appear in your list. (You can also click "Delete" next to any existing category to remove it).

---

## 📸 How to Upload a Portfolio Project / Case Study
*This creates a new gallery on your Portfolio page.*

1. Click on the **Projects** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Project Title**, type the name of the shoot.
3. Click the **Category** dropdown menu and select a category.
4. Under **Year**, type the year the shoot took place.
5. Under **Editorial Description**, write a short paragraph about the shoot.
6. Under **Cover Image**, click the upload box and select the main image that will represent this project on your homepage.
7. Under **Case Study Gallery Images**, click the upload box. You can select **multiple images at once** from your computer. These will become the full photo grid for the project.
8. Wait for all the thumbnails to appear with green checkmarks.
9. Click the **Create Project** button at the bottom of the form.

---

## ⭐ How to Add a Client Testimonial

1. Click on the **Testimonials** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Click inside the **Client Name** text box and type their name.
3. Click inside the **Role / Detail** text box (e.g., "Wedding Client" or "Creative Director").
4. Click inside the **Quote** text box and type their review.
5. Under **Client Photo**, click the upload box and select an image of the client or from their shoot.
6. Wait for the green **Uploaded** checkmark.
7. Click the **Add Testimonial** button.
8. *(Note: If you have less than 6 testimonials, the website will smartly adjust the grid to only show the ones you have created).*

---

## 💼 How to Add Services & Pricing

### Adding a Service
1. Click on the **Services** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Type the **Service Name** (e.g., "Elopements").
3. Type a short **Description** of the service.
4. Click the upload box to add an **Image** for the service.
5. Click the **Add Service** button.

### Adding a Pricing Package
1. Click on the **Pricing** tab in the left sidebar.
2. Type the **Package Name** (e.g., "The Premium").
3. Type the **Price** (e.g., "$1,500").
4. Under **Features**, type a feature (e.g., "4 Hour Coverage") and click **Add Feature**. Repeat this for every feature included in the package.
5. Once all features are added, click the **Create Package** button.

---

## ⚙️ How to Change Global Settings (Contact Info & Social Media)
*This controls the links in your website's Footer.*

1. Click on the **Settings** (or Config) tab in the left sidebar.
2. To change your email, click the **Contact Email** box and type your new email.
3. Do the same for **Contact Phone** and **Studio Address**.
4. To link your social media, find the **Instagram URL**, **Behance URL**, etc., and paste the **full web address** to your profile (e.g., `https://instagram.com/yourhandle`).
5. Click the **Save All Settings** button at the top right.

---

## ⚠️ Important Note on Uploading Images
- **You do NOT need to shrink or compress your images!** 
- Your website uses an automatic AI optimization tool. When you upload a massive, high-resolution photo, the website automatically converts it behind the scenes so it loads instantly for your clients without losing any visual quality. 
- Simply click "Upload" and choose your best quality JPEGs!
